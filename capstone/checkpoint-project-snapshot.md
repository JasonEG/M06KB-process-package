## The Process

The subject process is **In-Home Dog Boarding Intake and Care** — the end-to-end workflow Abbey Cohen follows when a client requests overnight dog boarding at her home. The process covers everything from the first inquiry through the post-stay follow-up message, including client screening, information gathering, a conditional meet and greet, daily care routines, issue escalation, pickup, and follow-up.

The process has **9 phases** and two primary roles: **Dog Sitter** (Abbey Cohen, who executes nearly the entire process) and **Dog Owner** (the client, who is active at inquiry, drop-off, and pickup). There are three decision gateways with meaningful branch logic: one at inquiry screening (accept or decline), one at the meet and greet step (required for new clients, optional for established repeat clients), and a recurring set of daily care decisions that can trigger an escalation path.

The scope deliberately excludes payment processing, booking platform administration, and veterinary care delivery — these were confirmed out of scope by the process owner.
